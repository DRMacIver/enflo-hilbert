Failure of a local continuation principle in Enflo's invariant subspace argument

Small defect does not, by itself, guarantee that a constrained descent can continue. This note constructs explicit extremal states in a multiplication operator for which the neighbourhood imposed by the convergence constraint has defect bounded away from zero.

Read the paper (PDF, 13 pages)

The target is the continuation inference after equation (46) of Enflo's preprint, version 2. The model has invariant subspaces: this is a critique of that inference, not a disproof of the invariant subspace theorem. The paper explains the hypothesis matching and the limits of its supplementary reachability result.

The abstract obstruction is checked in Lean. The explicit admissible states and their asymptotic estimates are proved by hand.
